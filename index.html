Singapore live air & weather

Haze, rain and forecasts for anywhere in Singapore, straight from NEA's live feeds.

🗺️ Open the live map → PSI / US AQI / PM2.5 by region, rain radar, a 12-hour replay slider, 2-hour forecasts, temperature, and a “Should I go out?” planner for your route and time.

Data: data.gov.sg real-time APIs (NEA). Basemap & search: OneMap (SLA). Runs entirely in your browser with no API key; calls are queued to respect the public rate limit. For guidance only: follow official NEA advisories.
